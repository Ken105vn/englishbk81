DUONG LAM

VIETNAM · HA NOI
🏯
Duong Lam, one of the most ancient villages in Ha Noi, is situated in Son Tay. Visitors can get there from the centre of Ha Noi by car, bus, or even by bicycle. It is famous for its ancient pagodas, traditional houses, and temples. Besides these, visitors can observe the locals making specialities, such as keo doi (peanut candy), and then try them.

HOLLUM

NETHERLANDS · AMELAND
⛵
Hollum is one of the ancient villages on the island of Ameland, the Netherlands. Many visitors come to the village because of its historical and cultural values. It is full of fascinating sights, such as traditional houses, a museum, a church, a lighthouse. Visitors can also take part in sports like kite-flying, surfing. Visitors can reach the village by air ferry.
